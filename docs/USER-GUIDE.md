# FieldSync — Guide for Community Health Workers

> FieldSync is a **demonstration** app. Use only the practice accounts and made-up patients
> your trainer gives you. Never enter real patient information.

This guide explains how to collect forms with FieldSync, with or without network.
The words in **bold** are the words you see on the screen.

---

## 1. The three things to remember

1. **You do not need network to work.** Every answer is saved on your phone as you type.
2. **Saved forms send by themselves** when the phone finds network. You do not need to wait.
3. **Never erase FieldSync or clear the browser** while forms are waiting to send.
   Forms that have not been sent will be lost.

---

## 2. Before your first day

You need network **one time**, for your first sign-in. Do this at the clinic or the county office.

1. Open FieldSync in Chrome. Your trainer gives you the address.
2. **Install it.** Open the Chrome menu (⋮) and tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
   FieldSync now has its own icon. An installed app keeps your saved forms safe;
   if you do not install it, the phone may delete them to free space.
3. Tap the FieldSync icon. Type your **Username** and **Password**, then tap **Sign in**.
   The first sign-in takes a few seconds: FieldSync is creating the lock for your forms.
4. Wait until you see **FieldSync is ready to work offline on this phone.**
   The phone has now downloaded everything it needs. You can go to the community.

**Optional: set a PIN.** Go to **Settings → Security → Set a 6-digit PIN**. A PIN lets you
open FieldSync quickly after it locks itself. You still need your password every 12 hours.

---

## 3. Filling in a form

1. On the **Home** screen, tap **New form**.
2. Check the **Facility**, then choose the form, for example **Sick child visit**.
3. Answer the questions. Tap **Next** to go to the next step.
   - The top of the screen shows **Step 1 of 3**, **Step 2 of 3**, and so on.
   - Some questions only appear after certain answers. That is normal.
   - If an answer is missing or not possible (for example an age that is too high),
     FieldSync shows a message under the question. Correct it, then tap **Next** again.
4. On the last step, tap **Save form**.
5. You see **Saved on this phone** and a **Receipt code**. Write the receipt code in your
   register if your supervisor asks you to. You can now start a **New form** or go **Home**.

**If you are interrupted** (a call, a patient, the battery is low), just leave. Your answers
are already saved. Tap **Close (keeps your answers)** or close the app. The form waits on the
Home screen under **Unfinished forms** — tap **Continue** to finish it later.

---

## 4. Working without network

When there is no network you see **You are offline** at the top. Keep working normally.

- Every form you save waits on the phone, locked with your password.
- When the phone finds network again — even for a short time — FieldSync starts sending.
  On most Android phones this happens **even when FieldSync is closed**.
- You can also open **Sync** and tap **Send now**.

The Home screen tells you how things stand:

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| **All forms sent** | The server has every form. |
| **3 forms waiting to send** | Saved on the phone, not sent yet. They will go when there is network. |
| **Sending…** | Sending now. Keep the app open if you can. |
| **No connection — forms are safe on this phone** | Nothing to do. Forms will go later. |

---

## 5. What the labels mean

Open **Sync** to see every form that has not been sent yet.

| Label | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Waiting** | Saved, not sent yet. | Nothing. |
| **Sending** | Going to the server now. | Nothing. |
| **Will retry** | The network failed. FieldSync will try again by itself (**Next try …**). | Nothing. Tap **Try now** if you have good network. |
| **Sent** | The server has received it. | Nothing. Sent forms stay in the list for 30 days. |
| **Needs fixing** | The server could not accept it. | See section 6. |
| **Changed on server** | Your supervisor changed this form after you sent it. | See section 6. |

A form is never sent twice. If the network breaks in the middle, FieldSync tries again,
and the server keeps only one copy.

---

## 6. When a form needs your attention

The Home screen says **1 form needs your attention**. Open **Sync**, then tap the form under
**Needs your action**.

### "Needs fixing"

The screen says **The server could not accept this form**.

- If it lists answers under **Please check these answers**, tap **Fix and send again**,
  correct those answers, and tap **Save form**.
- If it says **This form type has been replaced by a newer version**, tap
  **Fix and send again**. Your answers move to the new version. Check every step — there may
  be a new question — then tap **Save form**.
- For any other message, show the **Support code** (for example **FS-1A2B3C**) to your
  supervisor. Do not delete the form unless your supervisor tells you to.

### "Changed on server"

The screen says **Someone changed this form on the server**. It shows **Your answer** and the
**Server answer** for every question that is different.

- Tap **Combine both (recommended)** in most cases. It keeps your supervisor's changes and
  adds yours.
- If you and your supervisor changed **the same** question, FieldSync asks you to choose:
  **Keep my version** or **Use the server version**. If you are not sure, ask your supervisor.

---

## 7. Messages you may see

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Sign in again to send your saved forms. They are safe on this phone.** | Your sign-in expired while you were away. When you have network, tap **Sign in again** and type your password. Then the forms send. |
| **Phone storage is full. Send your saved forms or free some space.** | You stay on your form and your answers are kept. Send your saved forms (they leave the phone once sent), or delete photos or videos you do not need. Then tap **Save form** again. |
| **FieldSync is locked** | FieldSync locks itself after 5 minutes without use. Type your PIN or password. |
| **A new version of FieldSync is ready.** | Tap **Update now** when you are not in the middle of a form. If you are, the update waits until you save. |
| **This phone was removed from FieldSync.** | Your supervisor removed this phone (for example after it was reported lost). Talk to your supervisor. |
| **Too many wrong passwords. Wait … seconds.** | Wait, then type your password carefully. |

---

## 8. Sharing a phone

Each person signs in with **their own** account. Each account's forms are locked separately,
with that person's password.

- To hand the phone to a colleague: **Settings → Switch user**. Your forms stay on the phone,
  locked, and keep waiting to be sent the next time you sign in.
- Never tell anyone your password, and never sign in for someone else.
- **Do not** use **Sign out and erase this phone's data** to switch users. That removes your
  forms from the phone. If forms have not been sent, FieldSync asks you to type how many will
  be lost — stop and send them first.

---

## 9. If your phone is lost or stolen

Tell your supervisor **the same day**. They will remove the phone from FieldSync.

Your saved forms are encrypted: nobody can read them without your password. Forms that were
already **Sent** are safe on the server. Forms that were still **Waiting** on the lost phone
cannot be recovered — another reason to send forms whenever you have network.

---

## 10. Other settings

- **Language:** **Settings → Language**. *Kpelle* is a placeholder in this demonstration:
  it shows English text marked ⟦kpe⟧, not a real translation.
- **Bright sunlight:** **Settings → Appearance → Outdoor (high contrast)**.
- **Device check:** **Settings → Device check** tests whether this phone is ready to work
  offline. Run it if your supervisor asks you to.
- **Training:** **Settings → Offline sync simulator (training)** lets you practise sending
  with bad network. It only uses made-up forms.

---

## 11. Getting help

When something goes wrong, FieldSync shows a **support code** like **FS-1A2B3C**.
Read it to your supervisor. The code helps them find the problem without seeing any
patient information.
